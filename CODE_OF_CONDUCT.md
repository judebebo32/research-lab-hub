# Code of Conduct
**Lab Protocol and Academic Activity Tracking Platform**

## Our Commitment

The Lab Protocol and Academic Activity Tracking Platform is an open-source project designed to help researchers, students, and academic professionals document laboratory activities, manage experimental protocols, securely organize research data, monitor research progress, and track academic and professional milestones, including tenure and promotion activities.

We are committed to creating an inclusive, respectful, transparent, and collaborative environment where users and contributors from different backgrounds, disciplines, institutions, and levels of experience feel welcome and valued.

Our goal is to make research documentation and academic progress tracking more accessible, organized, reproducible, and effective while promoting responsible research practices and professional development.

## Our Core Values

**1. Respect and Inclusion**

We welcome contributions from students, researchers, educators, developers, and academic professionals regardless of race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, disability, age, career stage, or educational background. Everyone deserves to be treated with dignity and respect.

**2. Research Integrity and Accountability**

We encourage accurate and transparent documentation of laboratory activities, experimental procedures, research outcomes, and academic accomplishments. Fabrication, falsification, plagiarism, deliberate misrepresentation of research records, or unauthorized modification of another user's data are unacceptable.

**3. Collaboration and Knowledge Sharing**

We believe that open-source development and responsible knowledge sharing can improve research productivity and accessibility. Contributors are encouraged to exchange ideas, provide constructive feedback, recognize the contributions of others, and support users with different levels of technical expertise.

**4. Privacy and Confidentiality**

Research data, student records, unpublished findings, intellectual property, and academic performance information must be handled responsibly. Contributors and users must respect data ownership, institutional requirements, and applicable privacy policies. Accessing, sharing, or disclosing confidential information without authorization is prohibited.

**5. Transparency and Continuous Improvement**

We encourage open communication about technical limitations, software errors, research documentation challenges, and opportunities for improvement. Feedback and constructive criticism should focus on improving the platform rather than criticizing individuals.

## Expected Behavior

All community members, including users, students, researchers, maintainers, and external contributors, are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome diverse perspectives and constructive discussions.
- Support accurate research documentation and responsible data management.
- Respect individual privacy, intellectual property, and institutional research policies.
- Acknowledge contributions and give appropriate credit.
- Report security vulnerabilities and inappropriate conduct through appropriate channels.
- Provide constructive feedback and assist others in learning and using the platform.
- Respect the professional relationships between students, supervisors, and academic collaborators.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, bullying, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Offensive, discriminatory, or sexually inappropriate comments or communications.
- Deliberate falsification, deletion, or manipulation of research or academic records.
- Unauthorized access to research data, student information, or user accounts.
- Disclosure of confidential information without permission.
- Plagiarism, misrepresentation of contributions, or misuse of another person's intellectual property.
- Retaliation against individuals who raise concerns or report violations.
- Attempts to exploit software vulnerabilities or compromise platform security.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including the GitHub repository, issue discussions, pull requests, online community interactions, documentation, development meetings, and other official project communication channels.

It also applies to in-person and virtual events organized as part of the project.

Users must additionally comply with applicable institutional laboratory safety, research ethics, data protection, and academic integrity policies. This Code of Conduct does not replace those requirements.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Anyone who experiences or witnesses behavior that violates this Code of Conduct is encouraged to report the concern confidentially.

**Official reporting contact: judebebo32@gmail.com**

Reports should include a description of the incident, when and where it occurred, and any relevant supporting information.

All reports will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with discretion. Information will be shared only with individuals who reasonably need it to address the concern, subject to applicable legal and institutional obligations.

Where a complaint involves the project maintainer or presents a conflict of interest, the reporting individual may contact an appropriate independent institutional office, such as an ombudsperson, research integrity office, or equity office. An independent project reporting contact will be identified when available.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation is strictly prohibited.

Depending on the nature and severity of the violation, enforcement actions may include:

- A private warning and request for corrective action.
- Removal of inappropriate comments, contributions, or content.
- Temporary suspension of participation.
- Permanent exclusion from the project community.
- Referral to relevant institutional authorities when necessary.

The reporting individual will receive an acknowledgement and updates on the review process when appropriate. Reports will be handled as promptly as reasonably possible, taking account of their complexity and severity.

## Continuous Review

This Code of Conduct will be reviewed at least every six months. Feedback from community members will be considered to improve the document and support an inclusive, responsible, and welcoming research community.

## Our Shared Responsibility

We believe that technology should make research more transparent, academic collaboration more effective, and professional development more accessible. By participating in this project, every member of our community contributes to an environment built on respect, integrity, accountability, and shared learning.
